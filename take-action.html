Take action

No one can do everything, but everyone can do something. Choose your lane — and start today.

Personal

Start with your own two hands

Switch to clean energy

Choose a renewable tariff, install rooftop solar, or join a community energy scheme.

Eat more plants

Cutting meat and dairy is one of the biggest single ways to lower your footprint.

Reduce & repair

Buy less, fix more, and give products a second life before they reach landfill.

Talk about it

Climate silence is the enemy of action. Start the conversation with friends and family.

Community

Multiply your impact with others

Join a local group

Find a climate, conservation, or transition-town group near you and show up.

Plant trees

Volunteer at a tree-planting day — or organise one in your own neighbourhood.

Run a cleanup

Organise a river, beach, or street cleanup. Small effort, visible results.

Start a project

Community garden, bike scheme, solar co-op — we can help you get it off the ground.

Advocacy

Change the system, not just yourself

Vote for climate

Make climate policy a deciding factor in every election, local and national.

Write to leaders

A short, respectful letter to your representative carries real weight. We'll show you how.

Divest & invest

Move your money from fossil fuels toward clean energy and green funds.

Join a march

Add your voice — and your body — to the millions demanding urgent, just action.

Make a Pledge

Commit to one action this year

Public commitments stick. Tell us what you'll do — and we'll follow up with resources, reminders, and support.
