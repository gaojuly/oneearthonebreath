The roadmap

A phased journey from research validation to global community — organised by years from project commencement.

Year 1 · Genesis

Research & validation

Develop and test an initial MMI with academic collaborators, including wavelet-entropy research. Redesign the app experience and prototype contemplative art. Launch a Universal-tier minimum viable product with generative ocean sounds.

Year 2 · Expansion

Hardware & education pilots

Integrate compatible BrainCo FocusCalm hardware. Pilot the evening relay in three locations — Hong Kong, London, and New York. Introduce STEM+ modules in 50 pilot schools.

Year 3 · Global Pulse

Community growth

Extend the Mindfulness Hour to 12 regional time-zone groups. Launch the community hub with a live globe visualisation. Release the Terra Nova neurofeedback game.

Year 4+ · Integration

Research collaboration & wider access

Explore governed access to consented research data through an API for beneficial AI research. Expand charitable delivery in Africa and the wider Global South. Explore a global neurofeedback challenge event.
