Our Mission

The air we breathe connects us all

Climate change doesn't respect borders. The carbon you emit becomes the air your neighbours — and strangers on the other side of the planet — breathe. That shared atmosphere is our common ground.

  • Cut greenhouse gas emissions through community-led action
  • Restore forests, wetlands, and oceans that absorb carbon
  • Champion clean air and climate justice for the most vulnerable
  • Educate and empower the next generation of earth defenders
What We Tackle

The crises we can't ignore

Six interconnected threats — and one planet's future on the line. Understanding them is the first step to acting on them.

Climate Change

Rising temperatures are destabilising weather, agriculture, and life itself. We push for the urgent emissions cuts science demands.

Air Pollution

Nine in ten people breathe unhealthy air. We monitor air quality and campaign for clean-energy transitions in cities.

Deforestation

Every minute, forests the size of 40 football pitches disappear. We restore native ecosystems and protect standing forests.

Ocean Health

Oceans absorb 25% of our carbon and produce half our oxygen. We fight pollution, acidification, and overfishing.

Biodiversity

One million species face extinction. Healthy ecosystems are our planet's lungs — and its safety net.

Climate Justice

The people least responsible for emissions suffer first and worst. We centre frontline communities in every solution.

What We Do

Turning concern into action

Real solutions, on the ground, in communities — led by people who live there.

Community Renewables

We help neighbourhoods fund and install solar, wind, and micro-grids.

Reforestation

Native trees, planted with local stewards, restored hectare by hectare.

Clean Air Monitoring

Open air-quality sensors give communities the data to demand change.

Youth & Education

Curriculum, workshops, and fellowships for the next earth defenders.
