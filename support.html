The Social Mission

Spiritual Oasis

A community's wellbeing includes those with the fewest resources.

We propose working with local charities and community organisations in underserved regions — including parts of Africa and the wider Global South — and with communities affected by displacement, incarceration, or social exclusion.

Practical support should respond to people's circumstances and be developed with organisations that understand their languages, cultures, and local needs.
